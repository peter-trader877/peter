A+ Signal Rules

  1. Do not trade simply because the scanner shows a directional bias.
  2. Do not enter solely because price reaches VAH, POC or VAL.
  3. Liquidity interaction should be interpreted together with market structure.
  4. Macro conditions provide context rather than guaranteeing direction.
  5. Wait for the planned lower-timeframe confirmation.
  6. Define invalidation before entering.
