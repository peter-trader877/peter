Setup Development

Follow the progression from market condition to potential trade confirmation.

1. Context

Establish higher-timeframe direction, auction condition and important levels.

2. Liquidity

Identify obvious external and internal liquidity that price may interact with.

3. Reaction

Watch how price behaves after reaching important liquidity or auction levels.

4. Confirmation

Wait for lower-timeframe market structure confirmation before considering an entry.
